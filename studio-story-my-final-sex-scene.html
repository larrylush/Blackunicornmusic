My Final Sex Scene

My final sex scene was on HBO. I was there strictly in a musical capacity.

By Larry Lush

John Truelove, Gene Serene and I were working together as Now Voyager. Gene sang, I played keyboards and did the programming, and Chris Harvey played guitar. John had already put out “You Got the Love” as The Source, with Candi Staton’s vocal, and that gave us a chance to come at the record from another angle.

We slowed it down, put it over a breakbeat and brought in piano and strings. Candi’s voice was still at the centre of it. That became the Now Voyager Mix. React put it out in 1997 and the single went to No. 3 in the UK. It’s a song that keeps finding its way back into people’s lives, whichever version they heard first.

Seven years later, the mix turned up at the end of Sex and the City. The finale was “An American Girl in Paris (Part Deux)”. Carrie was back in New York, and our mix played over the ending. After six seasons of complicated relationships, somebody still had to play the closing record. That was us.

Release and screen credits
