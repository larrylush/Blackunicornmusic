How our Blur and Prodigy bootleg went from white label to Parlophone

That’s me in the suit on the front cover. Dave Conway and I had invented a bloke called Brock Landars, an American porn star who’d made a record. That was the ruse. We made the record, and I got to play Brock on the sleeve. My acting career began and ended with that suit.

By Larry Lush

When Dave Conway, who ran Silver Planet Records, and I made this record, Blur’s “Song 2” and the Prodigy’s “Smack My Bitch Up” were both everywhere. I remember talk of bad blood between the bands. Whether there was anything in it or it was just the way the story was told at the time, there was something funny about putting them on the same record.

We took the break from the Prodigy record and put Blur’s “Song 2” over it, “woo-hoo” and all. We called it “Smack My Dick Up” and made up an artist name, Brock Landars. It started circulating as a white label. London’s Xfm got a copy and kept playing it.

Then Parlophone picked it up. The label that had released “Song 2” was now putting out the record we’d made from it, licensed from Silver Planet. It came out in 1998 as S.M.D.U., still under the Brock Landars name, with “Crazy Sadie” alongside it. It reached No. 49 on the UK Singles Chart that July.

The trade press even printed a backstory for Brock, the American porn star we’d invented, complete with a gambling debt. Dave and I just made the record. By the time it charted, the bloke we’d made up had a life of his own. He’d got to No. 49 without ever having to turn up for an interview.

Release details

  • Commercial release · Parlophone / Blue Planet, 1998. UK CD CDBLUE 001 and UK 12-inch 12 BLUE 001.
  • CD tracklist · “S.M.D.U.” (7-inch mix), “Crazy Sadie”, and “S.M.D.U.” (12-inch mix).
  • UK Singles Chart · No. 49 in July 1998.

The artwork and pressings

  • Parlophone CD single and sleeve credits
  • UK 12-inch single, 12 BLUE 001
  • Single-sided 10-inch white label, DIGGLER 01
  • Archive of 11 editions, including the promo 10-inch, cassette and international sleeves
