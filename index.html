BLACK UNICORN RECORDS

Independent Music Without Boundaries

Music without compromise.

Black Unicorn Records is an independent record label founded by Larry Lush.

Born out of more than three decades of electronic music production, the label is a home for creative exploration, melodic electronic music, ambient soundscapes, progressive trance, experimental electronica and everything in between.

Alongside new material, Black Unicorn Records celebrates its musical heritage through carefully curated archive releases, remixes, previously unreleased recordings and limited-edition physical releases.

Our philosophy is simple: artistic freedom, creative independence and a direct connection between the artist and the listener.

Through our sister brand, Black Unicorn Audio, that same creative philosophy now extends into the development of original audio software, virtual instruments and experimental production tools.
