BLACK UNICORN RECORDS

Independent Music Without Boundaries

Music without compromise.

Black Unicorn Records is an independent record label founded by Larry Lush.

Born out of more than three decades of electronic music production, the label is a home for creative exploration, melodic electronic music, ambient soundscapes, progressive trance, experimental electronica and everything in between.

Alongside new material, Black Unicorn Records celebrates its musical heritage through carefully curated archive releases, remixes, previously unreleased recordings and limited-edition physical releases.

Our philosophy is simple: artistic freedom, creative independence and a direct connection between the artist and the listener.

Through our sister brand, Black Unicorn Audio, that same creative philosophy now extends into the development of original audio software, virtual instruments and experimental production tools.

Found Horizons – Volume 2

Quietman

Rare remixes, lost recordings and studio experiments from more than three decades of Quietman.

Read more

Found Horizons – Volume 2 opens the vault once again. Spanning more than three decades of Quietman recordings, this collection brings together rare remixes, forgotten releases, tracks once heard only in live sets, studio experiments, alternative versions, lost recordings and demos from albums that never quite made it into the world. Drawn from original studio masters, archive recordings and forgotten corners of the Quietman catalogue, these are pieces from different chapters of the journey. Some may be familiar to those who've followed Quietman over the years; others have remained tucked away in the archives, waiting for another moment in the sun. From deep melodic journeys and atmospheric explorations to unexpected collaborations, alternative directions and beautifully strange studio detours, Found Horizons2 celebrates the many paths Quietman has travelled — including those that led somewhere unexpected. Not quite a retrospective. Not quite a rarities collection. More like discovering a parallel history of Quietman, one recording at a time. Thirty years of experiments, ideas, adventures and musical horizons. Some remembered. Some forgotten. Some never heard before. All part of the journey. Welcome back to the Quietman universe.
