MODERN PRODUCER STRATEGIES — OFFLINE DIGITAL DECK

QUICK START (Mac or Windows)
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
3. It runs locally and does not need an internet connection.

You can also open Modern_Producer_Strategies.html for a single-file edition
with all 51 card fronts, the reverse artwork and sound effects embedded.

FEATURES
- 49 numbered strategy cards + 2 wild cards
- Original reverse-side artwork
- Draw one card or draw a three-card spread
- Shuffle and reset with sound effects
- Sound on/off control
- Browse all cards and edit card text locally
- Restore the original deck at any time

Card edits are stored in the browser on this device. The supplied artwork and
sound effects are built in. No account, server or internet connection needed.
