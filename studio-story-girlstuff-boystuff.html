How we accidentally made the Girlstuff/Boystuff theme tune

A friend knew a friend who knew a friend. Then one Saturday afternoon, with a bit of downtime in Tall Paul’s studio at Turnmills, we ended up making a cartoon theme tune.

By Larry Lush

The introduction came through a chain of friends and led to a conversation about a new animated series. I remember Inspector Gadget coming up. The bit that lodged in my head was the connection between its theme and Grieg’s “In the Hall of the Mountain King”. Change the rhythm and suddenly a melody you know becomes the sound of a cartoon detective. I can’t remember who made the introduction at the other end, but the new show was Girlstuff/Boystuff.

By then, Friends, Lovers & Family had grown beyond the breakbeat records we’d been making. It had become a way of getting our mates into the studio and seeing what we could make together. On that Saturday at Turnmills, we had Wilf, Miss MC and a bunch of other friends in to sing parts. Everybody brought something to it. We had a laugh, and by the end of the afternoon we had a theme tune.

We sent it over and he loved it. When the show went out, there we were singing over the opening titles. The tune stayed with the show across its two seasons. I still love that it started with a chain of friends and ended up on television.

Series details

  • Girlstuff/Boystuff was created by Ruth Beni. Canada’s Decode Entertainment was among the production companies.
  • The series ran from 2002 to 2006 across two seasons. Published listings name Seedpod as the theme performer.
