Legal bases

Where GDPR applies, processing may be necessary to respond to requests or perform a contract, comply with legal obligations, or pursue legitimate interests in operating, securing and supporting the website, subject to your rights. Optional marketing and non-essential device storage require consent where legally required. Registration for beta testing is not blanket consent to unrelated marketing.

Optional website analytics

If you allow analytics, we measure page views, repeat visits, referral domains, campaign labels, Bandcamp and download clicks, device and browser type, and approximate country and region. Cloudflare receives the events and forwards these details to our Google Sheets report. The report uses a randomly generated browser identifier that expires after 90 days. Your browser stores your analytics choice. You can withdraw consent and clear the identifier through Analytics preferences.
