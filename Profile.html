PROFILE

A few highlights from 30 years behind the desk, in the studio and on the dancefloor.

Over the years I've been fortunate enough to work on productions, remixes and collaborations involving some extraordinary artists and names from across the musical landscape.

Blondie · INXS · George Michael · Pet Shop Boys · The Stone Roses · The Human League · Faithless · Duran Duran · Dannii Minogue · P!nk · Afrika Bambaataa · Candi Staton · Afro Celt Sound System · Mauro Picotto · Dario G · Camisra · Tall Paul · Riva · Push · Umboza · Hysteric Ego · The Source · and many more.

From major-label releases and international chart records to underground house, trance, breakbeat and experimental electronic music, it's been quite a journey.
A lot of this work happened behind the scenes, often in collaboration with other producers, artists and remixers. That's always been part of the magic.

I'm bringing that experience together now through Larry Lush Recordings and Black Unicorn Records, alongside new music, production services, archive releases and creative projects.

Thirty years of making music. Still creating. Still independent. Still curious.
Thanks to everyone I've shared a studio, a record, a remix or a dancefloor with along the way.
