Larry Lush

Laurence Elliott-Potter

Three decades of electronic music. Still independent. Still curious.

From the underground to the charts

Born in Plymouth, England, Laurence Elliott-Potter, better known as Larry Lush, has spent more than three decades making electronic music. His work spans ambient composition, experimental breakbeat, techno, progressive house and melodic trance, alongside production, engineering and remix work for the wider music industry.

His collaboration with Casper Pound as New London School of Electronics produced The Deepest Cut on Rising High Records. Friends, Lovers & Family and Quietman provided further outlets for his exploration of rhythm, melody and sound.

Lush Recordings — the Truelove years

In 1994, Larry and Mr Oz ran Lush Recordings within London's Truelove Label Collective, releasing ambient, trance, techno and experimental breakbeat, including work by Friends, Lovers & Family, Quietman and Mr Oz & Larry Lush.

Production and remix work

Much of Larry's work has taken place behind the scenes, collaborating with producers and remixers including Tall Paul and Mixmaster Morris. Projects include the Mixmaster Morris interpretation of Coldcut's Autumn Leaves and INXS's Precious Heart.

Selected artists associated with his production, remix and collaborative work include Blondie, INXS, George Michael, Pet Shop Boys, The Stone Roses, The Human League, Faithless, Duran Duran, Dannii Minogue, P!nk, Afrika Bambaataa, Candi Staton, Afro Celt Sound System, Mauro Picotto, Dario G, Camisra, Tall Paul, Riva, Push, Umboza, Hysteric Ego and The Source.

Quietman

Developed with long-standing creative collaborator Wilf Frost, Quietman combines expansive melodies, intricate rhythms and analogue textures. Releases through Platipus Records established the project within British melodic and progressive trance. Recent albums Trancestors and [RE]LOVE continue that journey, alongside live performances and rediscovered archive recordings.

Black Unicorn — the next chapter

Now based in central Portugal, Larry continues to compose, produce and perform through Black Unicorn Records, releasing new music and material from his archives. Black Unicorn Audio brings his studio experience into the development of creative audio tools, exploring the connections between sound, visual art and technology. He also shares his experience through production tuition, mentoring and artist residencies.
